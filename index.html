About

I've worked in finance for fifteen years, first in industry and since 2015 at Jaquillard Minns, where I've been Managing Director since 2019. Most of my work is virtual CFO, financial reporting and forecasting, for clients ranging from start-ups and families to listed companies.

I've always fixed finance problems with code, starting with Excel VBA models and spreadsheets that ran themselves, and now full products. Prepaidly and VendorAlert are the two I've shipped most recently.

The test I use for anything I build is plain. Does it make month-end shorter, and does it make the numbers easier to trust?
