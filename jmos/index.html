Building JMOS

JMOS is the operating system I'm building for Jaquillard Minns, our accounting firm in Adelaide. It connects the systems we already use so information is entered once and flows where it's needed, and people spend less time copying and more time on review, judgement and clients. It's internal, not a product. This is the story of building it, written as I go.
